SiteWatch

SiteWatch is a personal, non-commercial tool. It checks the websites and YouTube channels its owner follows and sends new posts to the owner's private Telegram chat.

Under each YouTube video it offers buttons that add the video to one of the owner's own YouTube playlists. It is used by its owner only and is not offered to other people.

Source code: github.com/abomhmmod/sitewatch
